Our mission

Head and neck ultrasound (US) plays a pivotal role, ranging from initial assessment of neck pathologies to being an important adjunct bridging the gap between clinical evaluation and CT/MRI, and so proves to be an important problem-solving tool in many instances. Furthermore, the role of US extends beyond this diagnostic ability and allows US guided interventions, both diagnostic and therapeutic.

Given the complexity of neck anatomy, dedicated training is required to master the ultrasound techniques, in order to utilise the strength of US to its full potential.

We want to help you elevate your H&N ultrasound skills.

Dr Gajraj Sharma
Course Director, LIVHNUS

About our course

This two-day course and workshop will help you develop a systematic approach to the scanning techniques pertinent to head and neck ultrasound and use the strengths of neck US to their full potential. Delegates will have plenty of opportunity to practise these techniques in hands-on workshops. Interactive lectures cover all aspects of neck US, from the constituents of the neck and anatomical landmarks to their associated pathologies. Throughout, you will work with a highly motivated and experienced faculty of consultants who are experts in their areas and part of tertiary level MDTs.

Overall, the course provides an all-round approach to head and neck ultrasound, including sampling techniques reinforced by hands-on practice sessions.

Benefits of the course

  • Hands-on scanning

    Dedicated small group sessions to practise US techniques.

  • Multidisciplinary panel discussion

    Expert panel discussion on difficult and challenging scenarios.

  • One stop clinic management

    Guidance on various aspects of running a “One Stop Clinic”.

  • Improve sample adequacy

    Cytopathology advice and tips. Practise hands-on slide preparation.

  • Interventions

    US guided interventions (FNA and core biopsies) on anatomical models. Also advanced techniques.

  • Interactive lectures

    Dive deep into neck US to master and learn neck anatomy, vital landmarks and pathologies.
