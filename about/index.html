About Dr Gajraj Sharma

Course Director of the Liverpool Head & Neck Ultrasound Course.

Background

Dr Gajraj Sharma is a Consultant Radiologist (H&N) at Aintree University Hospital, UHLG, and Course Director of LIVHNUS.

Need this: further qualifications, experience, areas of expertise and any verified achievements

Educational approach

The course is designed and delivered by head and neck radiologists and a multidisciplinary consultant panel with extensive experience of tertiary level head and neck cancer care and management. Teaching combines interactive lectures with small group, hands-on scanning and intervention workshops, so delegates practise the techniques they learn.
