Programme

9–10 June 2027 at the Radisson Blu Hotel, 107 Old Hall Street, Liverpool L3 9BD.

Sessions marked “tbc” are still to be confirmed.

Course structure

Day 1: Wednesday 9 June

Principles of ultrasound and neck sweeps, neck anatomy, salivary glands, thyroid and parathyroid glands, with a panel discussion and three hands-on scanning workshops.

Day 2: Thursday 10 June

Lymph nodes and neck levels, the metastatic neck, cytology and FNA, the One Stop Neck Clinic and interventions, with intervention skill-station workshops.
